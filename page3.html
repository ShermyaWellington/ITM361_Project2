Game Design

Game design is an area that combines creativity, technology, and problem solving. It involves creating the ideas, rules, characters, worlds, and experiences that make a game fun to play.

Why I Chose Game Design

My first plan was to become an architect, but that plan did not work out. Next, I've decided to go more into game design because I also enjoy playing video games and wanted to learn how they are created. Game design allows me to use my creativity while also working with technology.

What I Want to Learn

I want to continue learning about game development, programming, web design, and creating game ideas. I also want to improve my problem-solving and creative skills.

My Future Career

My goal is to work for a game design company after college. I would like to help create video games and work with other people to turn ideas into finished games.

My Favorite Types of Games

I enjoy playing different types of video games, especially games that have interesting characters, stories, or a lot of different things to do. I like games that let me explore, compete, or play with other people. Some of my favorite games to play are Roblox, Dead By Daylight, Fortnite, Identity V and many more. I get so immersed into these games because they are fun and creative.
